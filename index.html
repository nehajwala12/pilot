01 · The pilot

One evidence pack. Fixed scope, fixed fee.

CreditSwan takes one company — a live deal, a recent close, or a portfolio company — digests the data from its source systems, and surfaces what matters most. Then every number gets perspective: hundreds of third-party sources layered in, company performance set in the context of market and benchmark data, evaluated against comps — thousands of data points behind every exhibit.

Revenue quality

Gross-to-net decomposition, promo and discount dependence, price / volume / mix bridge across trailing periods.

Cohorts & retention

Repeat-purchase decay and cohort economics by vintage — how durable the revenue actually is.

Concentration

Customer and channel dependency, top-account drift, and where the book quietly narrowed.

Acquisition economics

CAC trajectory versus blended optics; contribution margin after fully loaded acquisition spend.

Margin & cash

Margin bridge, trade spend and deductions, working-capital and cash-conversion trend.

The scorecard

Agreed before kickoff: the exhibits we'll be judged on — plus a flagged gap list of what the systems couldn't answer. A flagged gap beats a guess.

Fit to the bookThe exhibit set flexes to the company. Consumer brands read on cohorts and CAC; distribution and multi-site platforms read on branch economics, customer concentration, and gross-margin drift — same pipeline, same rigor.
